Welcome to PedalPulse

PedalPulse is a web-based database application designed to explore historical Citi Bike trips, bicycle stations, and rider activity.

About Our Dataset

Our project uses Citi Bike trip records from September 2026. The dataset contains information about bicycle trips, station locations, bicycle types, and rider membership categories.

Database Overview

Total Trips: 106,294

Total Stations: 222

Data Source

Our dataset was obtained from the official Citi Bike System Data website.
